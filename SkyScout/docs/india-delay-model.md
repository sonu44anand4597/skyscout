# Indian delay estimation: data integrity and regional LSTM

## Existing production model audit

The installed US model is `delay-lstm-v2`: one PyTorch `nn.LSTM` layer with 48 hidden units, 0.2 dropout and a one-unit linear output. It consumes a sequence of ten earlier/scheduled airline-route observations, each with ten numeric features: scheduled departure hour sine/cosine, weekday sine/cosine, month sine/cosine, scheduled duration, route distance, prior route delay rate and prior route mean arrival delay. Airline and route are **not embeddings**; they select the history window. Feature mean and scale, route histories, route distances, Platt calibration, risk thresholds and held-out metrics are in `ml/artifacts/metadata.json`. The model is genuinely sequential, though it models airline-route history rather than an individual aircraft's rotations.

The source is official US DOT BTS Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance flight-level data, 2023–2025. The target is observed arrival delay **at least 15 minutes**; cancelled and incomplete flights are excluded. Training uses chronological 70% train, 15% calibration and 15% untouched test periods. Route delay history for each flight uses completed prior days, so same-day/future arrival outcomes are not model inputs. The local artifact's held-out ROC-AUC is 0.692 and Brier score is 0.1555; see `ml/artifacts/latest-evaluation.json` for the full recorded metrics. The production model does **not** use live weather or current airline OTP. Historical METAR/NOAA weather candidates are research artifacts only. `ml/app.py` serves `/health` and `/predict`; `backend/services/predictionService.js` caches results and sends risk-band notifications; the existing dashboard delay card displays the result.

## Indian source audit, 7 October 2026

- The [Ministry of Civil Aviation on-time-performance page](https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/index.php/time-performance/time-performance-1) publishes dated **airline-level aggregate OTP** for Indian carriers. The live page checked during implementation reported data for 6 October 2026. SkyScout retrieves it as clearly labeled operating context in the existing delay card. It is never converted into flight records, a probability or LSTM input. Its OTP definition and sample are not assumed equivalent to the BTS arrival-delay target.
- [DGCA monthly domestic reports](https://www.dgca.gov.in/digigov-portal/?page=jsp/dgca/InventoryList/dataReports/aviationDataStatistics/airTransport/domestic/monthly/index18.html) and [AAI traffic news](https://www.aai.aero/en/business-opportunities/aai-traffic-news) provide monthly airline or airport aggregates, not the scheduled and actual arrival timestamps of each flight needed for this target.
- [OpenSky's own FAQ](https://opensky-network.org/about/faq) says its movement data lack commercial schedules and delays; movement timestamps alone cannot label arrival delay. [OPDI data](https://www.opdi.aero/data.html) cover flight movements and events but do not provide a verified Indian scheduled-versus-actual arrival series for this training task.
- The [Indian domestic schedule dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/THULab/indian_domestic_airline_dataset) and [2024–2026 published schedules](https://dataful.in/datasets/14868/) list planned services, but no observed gate arrival times. They cannot produce arrival-delay labels.
- A [public CSV claiming 2025 Indian flight observations](https://github.com/TejShah804/India-Flight-Delay-Data-Analysis-Project/blob/main/india_flight_delays_2025.csv) contains January 2025 rows under Vistara (`UK`) and AirAsia India (`I5`). [Air India confirms Vistara's final flight was 11 November 2024](https://www.airindia.com/content/air-india/in/en/vistara-air-india-merger.html), and [AirAsia India's successor was merged into Air India Express under code `IX` on 1 October 2024](https://www.airindia.com/in/en/newsroom/press-release/air-india-express-aix-connect-merger.html). Its repository names only Kaggle as the source, without a dataset link or source-system provenance. These contradictions disqualify it as verified operational data.
- The [CC0 Kaggle Indian Flight Delay Datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/leofire123/indian-flight-delay-datasets) was downloaded and inspected on 8 October 2026 (150,000 rows, dated 2023–2025). It has no actual arrival timestamps or arrival-delay target. Its `Flight_Number` column uses fabricated two-letter prefixes matching the first letters of airline names (for example `IN8723` for IndiGo), and it contains 2,983 Vistara rows dated 2025 despite Vistara's 2024 merger. `Delay_Target` exactly equals `Departure_Delay >= 15` for all 150,000 rows. The file is unsuitable for the existing arrival-delay model, regardless of its public license or row count.
- [FlightStats historical flight status](https://developer.flightstats.com/api-docs/historical-flight-status/v3) advertises scheduled and actual times for individual flights, but access requires a Premium Contract Plan; SkyScout has no such data entitlement or credentials. No source-verified, licensed bulk Indian historical observations were available through the configured integrations.
- Unverified third-party CSVs and datasets without clear provenance or redistribution rights were not installed as training data.

**2026 aggregate Indian aviation data is available, but sufficient verified 2026 flight-level historical data was not available from the configured legitimate sources.** No Indian LSTM was trained. Indian training observations used: **0**. Indian model evaluation metrics and artifacts: **none**. The UI shows “Delay estimate currently unavailable for this flight” rather than a fabricated probability. Existing US probabilities remain on the US model.

## Prepared training path when legitimate flight records are obtained

`ml/prepare_india_flights.py` requires one observed flight per row with a flight number, airline, Indian domestic IATA pair, scheduled departure/arrival, actual arrival, distance and explicit cancellation status. All timestamps must include an offset; scheduled temporal features are converted to Asia/Kolkata time. It derives arrival delay from **observed** actual minus scheduled arrival, writes the ten-column schema used by the existing LSTM trainer, and records source URL, license, row count and raw/processed SHA-256 hashes. It rejects aggregate OTP, unknown airlines, unsupported routes, naive timestamps and invalid values. No processed CSV is manually edited.

After verifying source permission and enough chronological coverage, run:

```text
cd ml
.venv\Scripts\python.exe prepare_india_flights.py data\raw-india-flights.csv --output data\india-flights.csv --source-name "Verified provider" --source-url "https://provider.example/dataset" --license "Verified data-use terms"
.venv\Scripts\python.exe train.py data\india-flights.csv --region IN --output artifacts\india --epochs 8
```

The Indian artifact uses the **same LSTM architecture and ten-feature schema**, but a separate regional dataset, preprocessing, calibration and evaluation. Training refuses an absent or digest-mismatched provenance manifest and refuses output to the US artifact directory. It installs an Indian serving artifact only if the existing held-out gate (ROC-AUC at least 0.55 and positive Brier skill) passes; otherwise the candidate remains unserved. `/health` reports US and India model availability separately. The backend selects the region from both resolved airport country codes, and a validated Indian artifact can be selected automatically after the ML service loads it. Unknown Indian carriers are not mapped to unrelated carriers.

The current inference code still requires an exact airline-route history key. Therefore, the prepared Indian path does **not** yet satisfy the requested unseen-route generalization, even if a valid artifact were added. Once verified Indian records are obtained, this must be addressed with a trained broader-history/airport-airline feature strategy and evaluated on held-out routes before claiming national coverage.

The scheduled feature hour for Indian flights is IST. Current METAR/TAF reports for Indian airports remain in SkyScout's weather card; they are not passed to the installed LSTM because the production feature schema was not trained with weather. Current airline OTP is separate context, not post-hoc calibration. Flight-level LSTM metrics, represented airlines/airports and data dates must come from an actual Indian training run before any Indian probability is shown.

## Theme

The site uses `skyscout-theme` in localStorage, applies the `dark` class in `index.html` before React loads, and exposes an accessible light/dark toggle on the home navigation and dashboard, results and auth pages. Shared dark surfaces, typography, borders, controls and status colors live in `src/index.css`; the price chart uses theme-aware axes, line and tooltip colors. The preference survives route changes and account sign-out because it is local to the browser, independent of authentication.
